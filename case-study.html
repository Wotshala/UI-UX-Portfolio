DONUT WEBSITE

Designing an e-commerce experience that encourages bulk purchasing through smart UX patterns and a "Build Your Box" feature.

ROLE

UI/UX Designer

TOOLS

Figma

TYPE

E-commerce Concept

TIMELINE

3 Months

OVERVIEW

A local donut shop was transitioning from offline-only ordering to an online sales model. They needed a digital experience that would feel simple and authentic while encouraging customers to purchase in bulk—specifically boxes of 6 or 12 donuts at incentivized pricing.

The challenge: How do we make bulk purchasing feel like the obvious choice without sacrificing customization?

THE PROBLEM

FOR CUSTOMERS

  • Browse donuts without visiting the shop
  • Purchase individual donuts OR multiple flavors
  • Easily understand box options and pricing
  • Customize orders without friction

FOR THE BUSINESS

  • Establish an online ordering channel
  • Encourage larger orders (boxes vs. singles)
  • Increase average order value
  • Maintain the shop's local, approachable personality

THE CORE QUESTION

How might we help a local donut shop transition to online ordering while making bulk purchases feel like the preferred choice?

DESIGN APPROACH

I designed two distinct purchasing paths—individual and box ordering—allowing customers to choose what works for them. The key insight: don't force people through the same flow.

CORE PRINCIPLES

  • Visual hierarchy prioritizes images, names, pricing, and actions
  • Reusable components for cards, buttons, quantity controls, and box selectors
  • Simple, friendly personality—built for first-time online customers
  • Clear pricing to highlight box value proposition

USER FLOWS

PATH 1: INDIVIDUAL PURCHASE

Home → Browse Donuts → Select Product → Add to Cart → Checkout

PATH 2: BUILD YOUR BOX

Home → Build Your Box → Select Box Size (6 or 12) → Choose Flavors → Review Box → Add to Cart → Checkout

THE SOLUTION

The centerpiece: a dedicated "Build Your Box" experience that makes bulk purchasing frictionless and valuable.

KEY DECISIONS

WHY NOT PRE-SET BUNDLES?

Pre-set boxes limit customization. Customers already know what they like—letting them build it increases conversion.

WHY FIXED BOX PRICING?

Customers don't want to do mental math while ordering. A fixed price makes value instantly clear and removes friction.
